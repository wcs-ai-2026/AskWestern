Residence Fees and Payment

Residence Prepayment

Residence Prepayment

To secure your spot in Residence, all students are required to pay a Residence prepayment of $900. The deadlines differ for incoming first-year and upper-year students. Upper-year students apply for Residence on a first-come, first-served basis so the prepayment deadline depends on when the application is submitted.

Prepayments can be made in your myHousing Portal using a MasterCard or Visa credit card, or MasterCard or Visa debit card.

Additional Payments

For making further payments towards your statement of account, please follow the payment methods listed on the Office of the Registrar's website. Please note that your residence charges will be combined with your tuition charges on one statement of account in your Student Center.

Tenant Insurance

Tenant Insurance

Tenant insurance is a requirement for all students living in residence at Western. A fee of $85 plus PST is included with your residence room rates. Alternatively, if you hold an insurance policy that will cover you during your stay in residence, you are able to opt out. To opt out, you will be required to provide your policy information on the opt out form starting in late July. There is also a link provided in the Residence Onboarding Process. If you do not opt out before the deadline, enrollment is final and all premiums will be non-refundable.

A complete description of the coverage provided under the program including all terms and conditions, policy provisions, limitations and exclusions, can be found on the Marsh Canada website.


To report a claim, please contact either of the following:

Marsh Canada
Phone: 1-877-585-3458
Email: oncampusinsurance@marsh.com

International Programs Group (IPG)
Phone: 1-833-735-7525
Email: berkleyUtenants@ipgclaims.com

If students need claims advice or want to inquire about their insurance coverage prior to submitting a claim, students can contact Marsh directly at 1-877-585-3458 or by email to oncampusinsurance@marsh.com. Marsh would be more than happy to discuss your options and walk you through the claims process.

Internet and Orientation Week Pass

Internet

Internet service is included in your room charge at no extra cost.

Orientation Week Pass (OPass)

Having an OPass will allow you to gain access to all the events going on during Western’s Orientation Week. Most incoming students will not have to buy an OPass because it is included in your tuition under "Orientation Fee". To ensure that your Orientation Fee is covered in your Tuition, log in to Student Center, look under Detailed Statement of Account, and find Orientation Fee under the Ancillary Fees heading. For more information, view the OWeek FAQ.

Pay Your Fees

Please note the instructions below are for payment of your first and second installment of tuition, housing and other fees. They are not the instructions for making the residence prepayment. See the Residence Prepayment section above for information on completing the prepayment on the myHousing Portal.

 

How Much?

Costs are outlined in your Detailed Statement of Account. To find this:

  1. Log in to your Student Center
  2. Find the Student Financials tile
  3. Select "Statement of Account" (remember, this won't be available until mid-July)

 Fees include:

  • Tuition
  • Residence
  • Other, including your bus pass, Orientation Week pass, and health and dental plan

Learn more about your Statement of Account, including opt outs.

When?

Fees are due in two installments. Due dates are on your Detailed Statement of Account. 

  • August: Half your fees + extras
  • December: The rest of your fees

How?

Canadian Bank Accounts: 

  • Online bank transfer
  • Cheque/money order

International: 
International bank transfer using either:

  • Convera

What about OSAP, Scholarships and Bursaries?

Ontario Student Assistance Program (OSAP)

  • Apply for OSAP before June 30 if you need the money to help pay for your first installment
  • You may still need to pay some fees in August if your first installment of OSAP doesn't cover all of your fees

Scholarships
If you receive these scholarships, they will be deducted from your first installment in August:

  • Admission Scholarships for Black and Indigenous Students
  • Continue in Canada Admission Scholarship
  • National Scholarship Program
  • Welcome to Canada Admission Scholarship
  • Western Scholarship of Excellence

If you receive these scholarships, they will be deducted from your second installment:

  • Western Admission Scholarship
  • Western Scholarship of Distinction

Admission Bursaries

  • Complete the Admission Bursary Application by July 10 to apply for Admission Bursaries and Need Based Awards
  • If you receive an Admission Bursary, the funds will be deducted from your first installment in August
  • If you receive a Need Based Award, the funds will be deducted from your second installment
Payment Plans

Students wishing to set up a payment plan for their residence and tuition fees can do so by submitting an application on the Office of the Registrar Payment Options site. Select Flexible Payment Plan to find out how the payment plan works. The link to the application can be found at the bottom of this section by selecting Apply.

Before submitting a payment plan application, please allow a few business days for your residence fees to be applied to your Student Center Statement of Account. Otherwise, your payment plan will not include your residence charges. Please ensure any payment plan applications are submitted by the first installment deadline date found on your Student Center Statement of Account. Otherwise, you may be charged a late payment fee by Student Financial Services.
