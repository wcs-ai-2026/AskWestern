Student Services

At the Student Services office our goal is to provide current students with the resources to achieve their personal and academic success at Western Law. The Associate Dean (Academic) is available to meet with Western Law students, to listen to their questions and concerns in order to provide support and direct them to the appropriate resources available.

Please contact us for matters related to academic guidance, health & wellness, financial and student support.
