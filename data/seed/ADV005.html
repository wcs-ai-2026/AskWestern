    Welcome to Academic Advising

    Our Academic Advising team in the Faculty of Social Science is here to support your success at Western. We provide guidance on university policies, degree and module changes, special permissions, and program planning. If you’re facing academic challenges, we’ll help you explore options and get back on track. We value your privacy and will only share information with others if you’ve given permission. We’re here to help you achieve your academic goals.
