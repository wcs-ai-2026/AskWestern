No other undergraduate business program provides you with the opportunity to explore as many different career paths as the Ivey HBA. You are free to explore your passions beyond the classroom - and beyond the boardroom. You are free to create your own path and become whoever you want to be.

HERE’S WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

 

YOUR CLASSMATES

When you sit in an Ivey classroom, you'll be surrounded by some of the most accomplished, engaged and talented people you'll ever know.

PROGRAM STRUCTURE

The 2+2 program structure of the Ivey HBA offers you the opportunity to explore your academic skills and interests before considering career options.

CASE STUDIES

Ivey is one of a select few undergraduate business programs committed to the case-method of learning. You won't just listen to lectures and take notes. You will experience more than 300 real-world business cases during your time at Ivey.

CAREER MANAGEMENT

Gain access to a wealth of resources to help you explore career options, choose a personalized career plan and develop a strategy to get you there.
